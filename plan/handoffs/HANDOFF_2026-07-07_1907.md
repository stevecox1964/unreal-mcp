## Decisions locked in this session

- **Nobody hand-aims the map camera** — every capture repositions MAP_Camera over the bounds and
  pitches it −90 before firing; the BP must carry **no baked camera rotation**.
- **The capture component is one-shot** — created transient per capture and destroyed after the
  file write; a persistent capture component on the actor is a bug, not a feature.
- **Editor-actor commands resolve in the same world as capture commands** (`GetGameWorld()`,
  PIE-preferring) — aiming one world's actor and shooting another's is the class of bug to guard.
- The stale 2026-07-06 handoff docs were committed for chain history but are superseded — the
  backlog remains the single source of truth for queue state.
