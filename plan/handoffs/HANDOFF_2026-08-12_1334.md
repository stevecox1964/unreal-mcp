## Decisions locked in this session

- **Instruments before behaviour.** Three of five SR39 defects were readouts lying, not the model
  misbehaving. Before concluding an APC reasons badly, confirm the facts it was shown were true and the
  log recorded what actually happened.
- **Accepted is not moved.** The bridge answering "accepted" is not evidence the body moved; achieved
  displacement is stated separately, told to the model, and logged loudly.
- **One cell, one name.** `col,row` everywhere the model can see. `key` stays internal to SpatialMap.
- **A refusal is the APC's ruling, stored — never a code-side block.** `refuse_cell` changes what the map
  *offers*, never where the body may go, and the reason is mandatory because a bare "impassable" flag
  destroys the training signal (walkable-by-engine / refused-by-APC is the pair #53 wants).
- **No warning beats a goal.** SR39 has him naming the corn and stepping in it in the same sentence. The
  fix was making a refused cell *stop being work*, not adding caution text.
- **Give the model a map in the vocabulary we ask it to use.** The next-cell sense was keyed
  forward/left/right while doctrine said compass. SR39: 2 of 15 walks compass. SR40 after the rekey:
  **15 of 15**.
- **Avoidance is a reflex, not a plan** (#61). `llm_ms` is 8–10 s; `act_ms` is 150 ms. Cognition cannot
  steer collision avoidance. Reflexes sense and stop; the model routes.
- **Capture densely, interpret selectively** (#61). `bridge.capture_view` and `perceiver.perceive` are
  already separate calls — screenshots are cheap, VLM calls are not.

## Open questions

- **Does the forward trace actually hit anything?** SR40 logged `blocker: 0` across eight wedged ticks,
  but the gate was shut, so that number proves nothing about the ray. A mailbox is low and the ray may
  pass over it. **One live run showing a `blocker` line is the prerequisite for the whole reflex half of
  #61.** Do this before designing anything else.
- **`refuse_cell` has never been exercised.** SR40 avoided the corn entirely via #58's ground memory —
  the cells were already recorded `cultivated_field` from SR39, so the direction lines warned him before
  he stepped in. The refusal state is built and unused. Do not claim it verified.
- **292 reconnects per run.** No longer log noise (INFO now, `NoneType` warning gone), but the socket is
  being torn down and rebuilt constantly. That is churn, not a logging problem, and nobody has looked at
  why.
- **#61 needs decisions before building:** sampling cadence (distance vs time), **storage retention**
  (every 5 m over a long run is thousands of frames — decide before building, not after), whether sampled
  frames reach the decision or only the corpus, and whether the 4-heading survey stays as the canonical
  per-cell record (recommend yes — do not delete it as a side effect).
- **The remaining code-over-LLM overrides**, unchanged from last handoff: schedule travel rewriting the
  model's direction, `_bound_at_place_movement`, `_execute_routed_walk` substituting a route,
  `mark_blocked`, `_survey_travel_is_wedged`.
- Carried: #50 Sonnet A/B, #53 design, `AGENTS.md` deletion and `CLAUDEOLD.md` left untracked
  deliberately (user: *"Just leave them be"*).
