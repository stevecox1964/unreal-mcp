## Decisions locked in this session

- Scheduled travel has priority over opportunistic place surveying. Entering an unexplored cell while
  en route must not redirect the APC to that cell center.
- The accepted MCP_World logical lattice offset is X `0`, Y `650` cm. Image registration and logical
  grid alignment are separate transforms.
- Regrid is destructive to derived grid geography and images, but preserves authored positions,
  captured APC starts, bounds/image calibration, schedules, and ordinary memories.
- World setup order is: register map image/bounds → preview/accept grid alignment → Apply regrid →
  Sync world to reapply authored landmarks/places → optionally Capture starts with every APC correctly
  positioned → fresh sim run.
- `Capture starts` records every bound APC's current transform and must only be used while the sim is
  stopped and all APCs are at intentional wake positions.
- Route maps inform cognition but cannot be the movement authority. During a scheduled named trip, the
  deterministic navigation ticket/controller must own movement.
