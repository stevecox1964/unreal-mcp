## Decisions locked in this session

* **Entrapment is a missing MEASUREMENT, not bad reasoning and not a bail-out problem.**
  The user: *"It makes more sense to stop the bad behaviour instead of just blundering into a
  situation and expecting tons of code and system prompts to bail the APC out."* Correct.
  Do not add more prompt or more escape code — find the sense that was never taken.
* **"Before", not "instead of".** Foresight makes traps rare; it cannot make them
  impossible (vans drive off, doors shut). The #91 seal stays as the backstop. What changed
  is that prevention is now the main mechanism and sealing is the rare surprise — it was
  backwards before.
* **The vision LLM is the wrong organ for this.** "How many ways out does that spot have"
  is a measurement, not a judgement ([[architecture_lizard_brain_sensing]]). The engine
  answers it exactly; a VLM would guess — and guess wrong on the one case that matters,
  since a storefront opening and a passage look identical from outside. Enclosure is only
  visible from inside.
* **A continuous ring beats a predictive probe.** Sweeping from a point the body has not
  reached would need new C++ taking a world point. Not built, and mostly unnecessary: walk
  into a throat and the ring closes in front of you while the way out behind you is still
  open and still measured.
* **Radar measures empty air, NOT walkable ground.** Open water, a drop and a clear street
  all return "20 m open". Footing must keep doing that job; radar must never replace it.
* **Still facts, not fences** ([[feedback_facts_not_blocking]]). The radar recommends no
  heading and forbids none. Walking into a dead end on purpose stays the APC's call.
* **B is a speed fix only.** The radar sees all eight headings either way. After measuring
  the real cost (~3%), the plugin rebuild is a nice-to-have, not a rescue.
