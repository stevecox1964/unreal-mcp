## Decisions locked in this session

- **Stop grading live runs on locomotion.** A run's success criterion is now *did something social
  happen and did both parties remember it* — the Master Plan's own criterion, never once measured.
  Locomotion becomes a background service with a failure budget.
- **A gate needs a pass criterion or it is not a gate.** "Until X is reliable" cannot be satisfied;
  SR40's numbers (15/15 compass walks, zero `cultivated_field` footings, contiguous survey) are recorded
  as the measured bar that was already cleared.
- **#65 was deliberately built as a fact, not a recovery override.** The item specified "N stalls → code
  walks you out". That would add a sixth code-over-LLM override to the five the last handoff lists as
  problems *to remove*, contradicts [[feedback_facts_not_blocking]], and would pre-empt the live
  verification of #60's `tried_here`, which has still never been observed working. The wedge is now
  *legible*, not *bounded* — if the model ignores the fact, the stall still runs on. That is the thing
  SR41 measures.
- **Per-agent behavior belongs in `rules.md`, not the shared template** (MASTER_PLAN Part V rule 4). A
  surveyor who must not stop and a shopkeeper whose whole job is stopping cannot share one line.
- **Reuse over rebuild for Milestone 2** (#69): `episodic_memory.py` is already an append-only stream
  with a relevance blend. Add poignancy to *that* rather than building the `ConceptNode` SQLite design
  from scratch.
- **The survey is not being abandoned.** #61 stands as written and stays parked until a `blocker` line
  is confirmed. It just stops being the only thing that runs.
