## The audit findings (why this session mattered)

Evidence gathered from the live agent files, not inferred:

- `last_perception.json`: **every** character sighting is labeled `unknown person`.
- The same tick's `nearby_characters` carries exact engine truth (`{"name": "Maren", "distance_cm": …}`).
- The two were never reconciled; `SocialMemory` correctly drops anonymous labels.
- **No `social.json` existed for any APC, in any run.** `episodes.jsonl` had `saw: []` on every event.
- `memory.json` was four consecutive near-identical "Met someone new… asked about my missing hat" entries.
- **Zero occurrences** of `heard` / `spoken_to` anywhere in `agent_runtime/` — `speak_to` wrote a bubble to
  the engine and nothing else.
- Therefore both clauses of the prompt's reaction gate ("you see someone under People You Know", "someone
  is speaking to you") were **dead code**: the first gated on a permanently empty store, the second on a
  channel that did not exist.
- `speak_to` failed **4 of 7** times in the last run — cause now capturable via #42.

## Decisions locked in this session

- **Identity resolution is deterministic lizard-brain work, not a vision-model judgement.** Position + yaw
  decide who is in view; the VLM keeps describing appearance. Preserves the abstraction boundary — the LLM
  receives `display_name`, never an engine actor name.
- **Proximity is not sighting.** Someone behind the agent is not recognized, even at 2 m.
- **Anonymous sightings are not discarded wholesale.** An identified APC replaces at most one anonymous
  blob in the same bearing bucket; the rest survive because this world contains non-APC bystanders.
- **Out-of-earshot speech is consumed, not queued.** Overhearing a line one tick later from across the
  square would be worse than missing it.
- **#43 viewer question resolved as "both"** — dedicated components primary, recursive renderer beneath,
  raw JSON demoted to a collapsed advanced view. Claude's call under delegation; flagged open to veto.
- **Unsupported model claims are suppressed in code, not just discouraged in the prompt** (#40), and
  reported as warnings rather than silently smoothed.
- **Working style (saved to memory):** decide and build rather than presenting option menus; keep chat
  reports short; findings go to `plan/backlog.md`, not into conversation.
