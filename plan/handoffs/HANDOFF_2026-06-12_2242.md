## Decisions locked in this session

- **Lizard brain = "device driver" to the 3D world; it SENSES, never ADVISES.** The
  stuck note reports only the fact; the LLM figures out what to do. Prompt text that
  prescribes an action (e.g. "turn left") is out of bounds. (User-stated, load-bearing.)
- **Stuck detection belongs in code, not agent .md** — worlds may have imperfect navmesh;
  robustness is a sense, not a behavior rule.
- **Two-layer fix is intentional:** stuck-detection (software net) AND navmesh carveout
  (level fix) are both wanted; neither replaces the other.
- **`NavArea_Null` (not Obstacle)** for vehicle carveouts — removes cells so AI routes
  around, vs just raising cost.
- **Idle re-decide cadence = 4 ticks; stuck threshold = 3 no-progress ticks, 100 cm.**
  Tunable constants at top of `agent_manager.py`.
