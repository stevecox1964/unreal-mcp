## Open questions

* **How to fix the merge in `PlaceDB.refuse_patch`
  (`Python/agent_runtime/place_db.py:733`, the `max(radius_cm, row["radius_cm"])` on
  update)?** The rule "the radius only ever GROWS" is right for a wall re-proved by the same
  kind of evidence and wrong across kinds. It also **compounds**: the pre-SR53 row was
  already a 450 cm `measured` patch at `(-77, -2105)` — itself bigger than
  `dead_end.MAX_RADIUS_CM = 300` — so a fat radius has been eating small precise seals for
  several runs and dragging its centre with each one. Three shapes were considered, none
  chosen by the user:
  **(a)** never merge across `source` (`measured` merges only with `measured`, `stated` only
  with `stated`) — they are different claims with different TTLs, and merging conflates them;
  **(b)** clamp any `measured` row to `dead_end.MAX_RADIUS_CM`;
  **(c)** both. **I would do (c)**, and (a) is the part that stops the compounding.
* **What to do with the live fat row?** Shrink it to the 110 cm its own SR53 log states, or
  delete it and let the body re-prove it? Shrinking preserves a real reading; deleting is
  simpler and the body seals it again on the next bump.
* **Should `_radar_text` mark memory-vetoed headings?** At B the ring advertised
  "south 20.0 m — room to travel" and the order was then HELD at zero. The readout is honest
  about *air* and silent about *permission*, and Dufus spent a paid decision on it. This is
  the same class of lie #92 killed. Recorded as Open under backlog #94; not built.
* **Refused cell `8,4` ("American Bank building interior") blocks the whole 30 m cell.** It
  cost SR53 a decision the same way. Is a whole-cell refusal right for a building that only
  occupies part of it, or should it have been a patch?
* **`MEASURED_TTL_S = 1800` is STILL untested.** SR53 (301 s) and SR54 (230 s) were both far
  too short for a seal to lapse. Carried over unanswered from the last two handoffs.
* **`wake_to_first_displacement_ms` still misleads** (reported 62 s in SR51 for a body that
  moved at 7.6 s). Not started. Carried over.
* **`_RADAR_RANGE_CM = 2000`, `_RADAR_OPEN_M = 10`, `_RADAR_TIGHT_M = 3`** are still guesses.
