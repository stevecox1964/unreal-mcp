## Decisions locked in this session

- Every durable community and individual/owned place receives its own four-cardinal place image.
- Place-image pixels contain labeled N/S/E/W views but no grid or world-coordinate overlay; coordinates
  remain database metadata.
- Shared immutable image revisions are linked into each APC's chronological visual history rather than
  independently owned by one APC or named by sim run.
- Once a settled APC has a mapped scheduled-place image and textual description, routine changed-pixel
  VLM observation sleeps. Manual, schedule, movement, stuck/blocker, and proximity events remain valid
  separate cognition paths.
- A full PlaceDB reset deletes durable place-image records/files/history links; agent and day resets
  preserve place visual memory.
