**Root cause, confirmed in the C++.** `UnrealMCPCharacterCommands.cpp:346` computes the raster column
offset as `Right * (ColT * Radius)` with `Radius = 34` cm. **The scan spans exactly the body's own
width — ±34 cm.** `far_left` is 34 cm left of centre. So `open_columns` can only ever say "the gap is
inside my own shoulders", which is never useful for stepping around anything. Dufus was told
"no side is open" while clear pavement sat a metre either side, and the only move left to him was to
turn around. **The bounce is not a reasoning failure; it is a missing fact.**

### Built after this handoff was first written

- **#88 — turn the probe, not the body.** `AgentManager._open_headings` probes
  `get_character_forward_volume` at -90/-45/+45/+90 whenever the body does not fit ahead, and attaches
  `blocker.open_headings` (compass words, nearest turn first, measured clearance). **No rebuild:** the
  C++ already reads `yaw_offset_deg` (`UnrealMCPCharacterCommands.cpp:224`) and builds `ProbeRotation`
  from it (`:258`), and that rotation drives the **capsule sweep quaternion** — so an angled call is a
  real body-fit test down that heading, which is the same question walking asks. Chosen over widening
  the raster (a rebuild, and it answers the smaller question "the gap is 80 cm left") because **APCs
  steer by heading, not by raster column**. "None of them fit" is stated as its own fact, so being
  boxed in stays distinguishable from never having asked.
- **#89 — look as far as you mean to walk.** Prompted by the user asking *"I think we have a units
  problem. CMs?"*. The units were clean; the **scale** was not. One "step" was 15 m, a grown step up to
  90 m, and the probe reached **5 m** — the plan was sizing a move having measured a fifth of it. The
  5 m travel probe is deliberately left alone: it answers "is something about to stop me" and may WAKE
  cognition, so lengthening it would buy a paid decision for every wall down a corridor.
- **#90 — the engine's answer IS the step.** The user caught that #89 was still only half the job: the
  measurement was applied as a **cap on a 15 m constant**, so the constant was still the source.
  `plan_step` now takes `reach_cm` — *how far can this body travel along this heading* — and that is
  the distance, standoff subtracted. `_STEP_DISTANCE` survives only as (a) the fallback when nothing
  could be measured, and such a step **says out loud that it is a guess**, and (b) the hop length for a
  long step, because 15 m is what SR47 proved the navmesh honours.

## Decisions locked in this session

- **The move plan lives in the lizard brain, not in the prompt.** The model says only *which way*; the
  distance is computed from PlaceDB and the body probe. Per the user: *"Too much rule tweaking. Make
  the lizard brain figure it out."*
- **A grown step is never handed to the engine whole.** It is cut into hops of one nominal step and
  walked one hop per tick with no model call. The engine only ever sees a distance the fixed 15 m step
  always walked correctly. This is what makes `MAX_STEP_CM = 9000` safe — not the number itself.
- **The spec's "cap at the remaining distance to goal" was deliberately not built.** The goal-bearing
  paths (routed legs, sweep centres) walk to exact coordinates and never overshoot; the paths that DO
  overshoot are direction words, whose real goal is the refusal or blocker ahead.
- **Five things end a walk plan** (each buys a full cognition tick): something ahead the body must
  reckon with, drifting >7 m off the ordered line, anyone arriving/leaving nearby, a hop making no
  ground for `_STUCK_TICKS`, and a hard 12-tick ceiling. A survey outranks a walk; an operator pulse
  ends the plan, because "pulse" must still mean "think now".
- **#87 (lizard brain as an LLM) stays analysis-only, parked behind the overnight run.** It doubles
  per-tick cost, and if a model is ever needed there the residual questions are *visual*, so it would be
  a VLM. If built, it may **shape** a move (shorten, nudge) but never **refuse** one.
- **Speed mode was lifted for this lane** (user, mid-session: *"implement tests and code you don't
  need my input on"*). `test_move_plan.py` pays off the whole #86–#90 ledger; the suite is 65/65.
  `_scan_ahead` is now pinned end to end against a real `PlaceDB` too.
