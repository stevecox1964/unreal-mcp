## Decisions locked in this session

- **Place name wins over compass observations in the prompt**: When `known_place` is set, the `Place:` line shows only the formal name. Compass landmark observations are suppressed there (they can contradict the place name if a landmark was named "motel area" as free text). Rationale: observed in live run — landmark label beat the authoritative name and caused a bolt.
- **Schedule drives behavior, not a "stationary" flag**: Maren's rules say "follow your schedule." She can walk when the schedule says to (lunch, going home). She stays when the schedule says to (08:00–12:00 at truck). There is no hardcoded "she never walks" rule.
- **`reset_runtime_state` runs at every sim start (not just `reset_agents`)**: Ensures clean goal + timers without requiring a manual reset call between runs.
- **Auto-reposition on sim start, but preserve memories and place cells**: `start_simulation` teleports agents back to start transforms. It does NOT call `memory.reset_memories()` or delete `spatial_map.json` — those only happen in explicit `reset_agents`.
- **Personal familiarity is the primary "am I home?" signal**: `named_by_me=True` + high visit count is what tells the agent "this is YOUR place." Shared map labels alone are not sufficient.
