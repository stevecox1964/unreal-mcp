## What was done this session

Five live runs, each exposing a real bug. In order:

- **SR33 → the survey was photographing cells it was not standing in** (`bb2a1ae`). Dufus never
  moved from `(-10491.7, 718.6)` — cell (5,6) — yet wrote `community:5:5` rev 4 *and*
  `community:5:6` rev 2 from that one spot. Two causes: arrival was a distance test with the
  explorer tolerance widened to a full cell (#48), and a persisted survey interrupt keeps its target
  across ticks *and runs*. Containment is now the authority (`agent_manager.py` `_sweep_step`), and
  `_save_place_visual` refuses frames whose capture point is in another cell. `place_images` gained
  `captured_x`/`captured_y`. A 4-tick travel budget prevents containment becoming an infinite walk;
  exhausting it marks the cell blocked via `SpatialMap.mark_blocked`, which the sweep start gate now
  honours.
- **Decision log made auditable** (`bb2a1ae`) and **surfaced** (`fad2da7`). SR33 logged two walks as
  bare `walk_to success` with the same thought — one walked 15 m *into* the corn, the other back
  out. Rows now carry `at`/`cell`/`footing`/`facing_yaw`/`moved_cm` and
  `move:{intent,target,heading,distance_cm}`. PIE shows
  `walk_to north 15m ->N [success] | cell 4,6 | cultivated_field`; the sim-page feed shows the same
  plus interrupt rows that used to render as a bare dash.
- **SR34 → the survey left the avatar facing north, so "back" walked deeper in** (`52865c0`). Proven
  from the engine's own rotation values: `_execute_sweep_observe` turned through E/S/W/N and never
  turned back, and `compass_headings()` ends on N — so every survey left the avatar hard-locked at
  yaw -90, and every facing-relative word was then measured from north. `_wake_sweep` had always
  restored its facing; the cell survey never did. Fixed by restoring the entry facing, adding
  absolute compass directions to `walk_to`, and recording the heading actually travelled as a
  `Travel:` fact so "the way I came" is stateable at all.
- **SR35 verified that fix live**: facing restored to NE/-30, N/-78, NW/-156 (never north). The model
  switched to compass words on its own and **every retreat went the right way on the first try** —
  the corn-field decision that walked him deeper in SR34 walked him out. 3 new cells.
- **SR35's one LLM error → the model ran out of tokens mid-thought** (`c56b7e8`). `max_tokens=1024`
  caps thinking *and* response text together and Sonnet 5 thinks by default, so a tick could spend
  the whole budget reasoning and never write a decision. Now 4096 with `thinking: adaptive` +
  `effort: medium` declared. Request shape verified against the live API.
- **The survey seized a third of Dufus's ticks** (`3f0e3d1`) — see Decisions.
- **SR37 → we were deleting his survey reasoning, and the footing rule had no exit** (`2d27b61`).
  See Decisions and In flight.

Also: **full world reset** performed (DB wiped via the cockpit buttons, 318 observation frames and
both logs cleared by me). Authored places regenerate from `places.json` + `Landmark_*` actors, which
was verified — nothing authored was lost.

## Decisions locked in this session

- **The LLM is the boss for navigation; code carries out and reports.** User, verbatim: *"I don't
  care what nav mesh says. LLM is the boss."* Movement itself was already obeying — every ordered
  move in SR35 landed within 35–55 cm. The overrides were all in our code.
- **Surveying is an action the model chooses (`survey_here`), not an interrupt code fires.** Code
  used to seize the tick the moment it saw an unsurveyed cell — 12 of SR35's 35 ticks ran with the
  model's own action discarded, and code, not the model, picked which ground got mapped. What stays
  deterministic: the four headings, their order, the capture, containment, and the durable interrupt
  record. *Which compass point to shoot next is execution, not a decision.*
- **Compass directions are the default vocabulary; body-relative words are for reacting to the
  picture only.** The survey, the grid and PlaceDB's compass observations were already cardinal —
  facing-relative words were the only part that silently changed meaning underneath the model.
- **A composite may only be filed for the cell it was shot in**, and the capture position is stored
  so a mis-file is detectable rather than invisible.
- **Anthropic parsing must never assume `content[0]` is text**, and a missing text block with
  `stop_reason: max_tokens` is a *budget* failure, not a malformed response.
- **Rules that were correct when written become wrong when the surrounding design changes.** Both
  SR37 failures were this: the narration guardrail and the footing rule were each right before the
  control inversion and harmful after it. Expect more of these.
- **No handoff-time test-writing.** User, mid-session: *"Don't worry about tests!!!! I will test the
  code"* and *"once I verify the code actually behaves the way I want, then we can write tests for
  code that actually works."* Build → verify live → pin with tests, in that order.

## Open questions

- **Does Dufus survey more than once per run?** SR37 proved he *will* ask (cell 3,5 captured, which
  can only happen now if he chose it) but he asked once in 20 ticks, and spent the last third
  trapped in rough ground. Unresolved whether the low rate is the footing trap alone or also
  reluctance to spend four ticks.
- **Should rough-ground cells be surveyable at all?** The footing rule means a cell that is entirely
  grass/cultivated_field never gets captured from inside. That biases the corpus toward paved ground
  — and per the purpose memory, *skew is worse than a gap because a gap is visible*. Options: (a)
  leave it, accept paved-only coverage; (b) add a survey exemption so he may stand on rough ground
  long enough to capture; (c) capture from the nearest proper ground and accept an off-center view.
  **User decision — it changes the deliverable.**
- **The remaining five code-over-LLM overrides**, ranked and not started: schedule travel rewriting
  the model's direction (`agent_manager.py:3103`), `_bound_at_place_movement` clamping targets,
  `_execute_routed_walk` substituting a cell-by-cell route, `mark_blocked` deciding a cell is
  off-limits without telling the model, and `_survey_travel_is_wedged` giving up after 4 ticks. The
  agreed order was #1 (done) → blocking-as-an-LLM-declaration → stop rewriting destinations.
- **Bridge reconnect noise** — 269 of SR35's 2545 log lines are
  `Existing connection failed: 'NoneType' object has no attribute 'sendall'`. Harmless, still
  unscheduled.
- Carried: #50 Sonnet A/B evaluation, #53 design, `AGENTS.md` deletion and `CLAUDEOLD.md` left
