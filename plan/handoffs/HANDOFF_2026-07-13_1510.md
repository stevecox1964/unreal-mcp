## Decisions locked in this session

- Cheap engine/state sampling belongs below paid perception and cognition; stationary scheduled agents
  do not need a periodic paid model heartbeat.
- Unreal-specific capture and query mechanics stay behind an engine adapter. An engine-neutral visual
  cortex owns change detection, caching, image lifecycle, and VLM-request decisions; lizard-brain and
  executive layers consume engine-neutral facts/events.
- APC gaze/decision frames and place-survey images are different artifacts.
- A durable place survey is a shared geographic record with exactly N/E/S/W images plus level, grid XY,
  world XY, place identity/source, heading, capture time, and content hash/revision metadata.
- Place surveys survive agent resets, day restarts, and sim runs. A complete PlaceDB clean-out removes
  both their database records and files; an explicit refresh may replace them.
- A logical grid offset is a true regrid, not a visual overlay shift. Applying one must invalidate and
  rebuild all grid-keyed place, spatial, route, and place-survey data.
- Work is committed directly to `main`; no PR or autonomous backlog branch is part of the normal flow.

## Open questions

- For transient gaze evidence, choose overwrite-latest, a bounded ring, or content-addressed
  deduplication. Current recommendation: rolling scratch capture plus content-addressed blobs, with
  decision/sim-run metadata referencing the hash.
- How long should unique transient decision frames remain: last N runs, a time/size budget, forever, or
  manual promotion?
- Does every community cell get one four-image survey, and do authored/owned landmark anchors get a
  separate four-image set?
- Should the logical grid be adjusted only by drag/numeric controls, or also offer a heuristic based on
  landmark/street geometry?
- What actor radius/extent defines “arrived at the landmark” after reaching its community cell?
