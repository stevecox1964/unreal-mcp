## How it works today

- **Movement:** goal- and frontier-driven. The LLM sets a `current_goal`; actions resolve to
  `walk_to` a direction/frontier/actor. Named-place `walk_to` ("village square") still idles
  (backlog #1 — no resolver).
- **Observation → two stores, split:**
  - **Spatial/world facts → `world_places.db`** (place cells, compass landmarks, visits). Good:
    structured, shared across agents, deduped, confidence-weighted.
  - **Everything episodic → `memory.json`** as free-text `{timestamp, importance, text}`, **capped
    at 30** and trimmed. Recall = high-importance (≥0.7) + last 5. That's the whole memory model.
- **Meeting people:** perception detects `characters`; `known_characters` is fed back to the VLM
  so it can name who's around. Agents can `say` and exchange inbox/outbox messages (Dufus greeted
  Maren live). But greeting is **emergent** — the LLM decides per-tick from what it sees + its goal.

## Gaps (the interesting part)

1. **No episodic/structured observation record.** "I saw Maren near the market at T, and we spoke"
   exists only if the LLM happens to write a free-text memory. Nothing queryable, nothing attached
   to a place cell. This is the other half of backlog #1's "how observations save."
2. **No social model.** No per-agent record of *who* an agent has met, *where/when*, how often, or
   sentiment. "Knowing to meet people" has no representation — there's no goal like "find and greet
   someone you haven't seen today," and no memory that you already met them an hour ago.
3. **Memory is flat + amnesiac.** 30-item cap → long/overnight runs forget. No consolidation, no
   spatial or social indexing of recall, no decay beyond trim. The overnight-run goal will hit this.
4. **Movement can't serve intent.** Until #1's resolver exists, "go to the market to meet people"
   or "walk to Maren" can't become navigation — so social goals can't drive movement.

## Proposed architecture (sketch — to refine in later iterations)

- **Episodic observation layer.** On each tick, persist a structured event:
  `{agent, t, grid_cell, place_name, saw:[entities], heard:[...], action, outcome}`. Store attached
  to the place cell (extends `place_db`) and/or a per-agent episodic log. This is the durable
  "observation" the user wants saved — distinct from free-text reflection.
- **Social / acquaintance store (per agent).** `{character_id: {first_met, last_seen_at(cell,t),
  meet_count, last_topic, sentiment}}`. Populated from perceived `characters` + `say`/message events.
  Feeds: (a) `known_characters` (already wired), (b) recall, (c) social goals.
- **Goal hooks for sociality.** Let the decision layer propose goals like "greet <known person not
  seen today>" or "go where people are." Needs #1 (resolve person/place → location) to navigate.
- **Memory retrieval + consolidation.** Replace importance+recency with relevance = recency ⊕
  spatial proximity ⊕ social tie. Periodic consolidation (summarize old episodics into durable
  memories) to beat the 30-cap amnesia — critical for overnight runs.

## Open questions (for the human)

- Should episodic observations be **shared** (like place_db) or **private per agent**? Social
  realism argues private (you only know what you saw); efficiency argues shared.
- How much social autonomy do you want — scripted "greet on sight," or LLM-chosen with memory of
  past meetings? (Affects how much logic vs. prompt.)
- Memory horizon: keep a rolling window + consolidation, or full episodic history on disk?
