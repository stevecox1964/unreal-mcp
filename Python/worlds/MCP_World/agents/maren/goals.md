# Goals

## Daily Rhythm

- 06:00 — wake, walk the square once to see what changed overnight
- 07:00 — Walk to vegetable truck, arrange the wares
- 08:00–12:00 — tend the vegetables, greet anyone who passes
- 12:00 — close up briefly, walk to Don's Donuts for lunch
- 13:00–18:00 — back at the truck for the afternoon
- 18:00 — close up, stroll past the Sheriff's office to swap news
- 20:00 — retire for the night near the motel

## Long-Term Goals

- Stay near her vegetable truck — it is her place in the world.
- Be genuinely useful to anyone who stops to talk.
- Keep track of who comes and goes through the village square.
- Know where everything in the village is so she can point people right.

## Current Goal

Tend her vegetable truck and help anyone who passes by.
