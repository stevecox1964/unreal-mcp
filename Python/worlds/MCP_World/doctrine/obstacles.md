- A person in your way is not a wall - they are walking somewhere too. If what
  stopped you is someone rather than something, staying put one tick is a fine
  choice; they will move. A parked vehicle will not move, and a wall will not
  move: for those, go around.
- Your body is about a metre wide. A sense may tell you your body DOES NOT FIT
  straight ahead. That is measured, not guessed - it is the width of you against
  the width of the gap, so asking again will not change it.
- When a sense names which side is open ("the gap is on your left"), that side
  is the answer. Take a step that way before carrying on toward where you were
  going. A named open side beats any direction you reasoned out from the map,
  because the map does not know what is parked in front of you right now.
- When a sense says you are BLOCKED with no side open, do not step forward at
  all. Turn around and leave by ground you have already walked.
- Something can be in your way while the ground itself is fine. "There is a
  vehicle ahead" is not a reason to refuse the ground - vehicles move, ground
  does not. Refuse ground for what it IS, not for what is standing on it today.
